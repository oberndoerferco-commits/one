# AI & competitor briefs — Oberndörfer Milano

Standing instructions for the weekly brief. A scheduled routine (Mondays, 07:00 CEST)
opens a fresh session, reads this file and the latest brief, researches the week, and
writes `research/ai-briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md` plus an HTML artifact.

## Who this is for

Oberndörfer Milano (oberndoerferco.com, Shopify Basic, EUR, based in Germany, ateliers
around Milan). Handmade full-grain leather: bags, mini trunks, watch boxes, jewellery
boxes, leather sofas / armchairs / poufs / trunk tables, bespoke commissions, and the
Trax NYC jewellery-trunk collaboration. One-person marketing. Socials: Instagram
`@oberndoerferco`, TikTok `@oberndoerfer`. Connected data: GA4, Search Console,
Google Merchant Center, Google Business Profile (via Windsor.ai). Higgsfield account
exists (free plan, 0 credits as of 2026-09-02).

Voice: quiet, material-first, plain about provenance (tanneries in Italy, France,
Germany; CITES-documented exotics; solid brass; hand-burnished edges; patina). The
house does not claim heritage it does not have. AI must never be used to fake the
"made by hand" promise (no AI-generated artisans, hands, ateliers, or fake product
textures presented as real).

## Sections every brief must have

1. **AI news this week** — only items that change what a small luxury e-commerce
   brand should do. Regulation (EU AI Act Art. 50 labelling), platform policy
   (Meta / TikTok AI labels), Shopify features, Google Shopping / search changes.
2. **Image & video generation** — new models or features, with a one-line "use for us"
   (e.g. sofa-in-room visualisation, hero-shot animation, background extension,
   dubbing founder clips). Note price and whether it keeps product texture and
   hardware intact.
3. **Competitors & peers** — what they did with AI this week: campaigns, tools,
   backlash, statements. Include one "lesson for us" per item.
4. **Post ideas for the coming week** — 5 to 8, each with format, platform, hook,
   and AI role (none / assist / generate). Tie at least two to real products or
   pages on the store and at least one to a seasonal moment.

Every claim carries a source URL and a date. Say "could not verify" rather than guess.
Do not repeat items already in an earlier brief unless something changed.
