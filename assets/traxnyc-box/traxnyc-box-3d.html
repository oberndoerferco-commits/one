One piece, one column, 285 mm long over the five rows. The top is 70 and the bottom 65 because the lid overhangs the front by the thickness of the front wall and the lap.

How it opens

  1. Lap — 170 × 40, folded off the front edge of the top. Lift it first; the magnetic catch is in its free edge. Closed, it covers 40 of the front’s 55 and leaves 15 mm showing below — your 4 cm and 1,5 cm.
  2. Lid — the top panel, hinged on the top/back fold. It swings back and the lap folds back with it.
  3. Front — 170 × 55, hinged on the front edge of the bottom. It drops forward once the lap is clear.
  4. Base — bottom, back and both ends. Fixed.

There is no separate bottom band. The 1,5 cm you measured is simply the part of the front wall the lap does not reach.
