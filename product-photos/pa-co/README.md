## Folders

- `stone-EDEAE4/` — primary set. Warm off-white `#EDEAE4`, soft contact shadow. Use on the website;
  set the product-card / page background to the same `#EDEAE4` so images blend seamlessly.
- `white/` — pure `#FFFFFF` versions for marketplaces (Google Shopping, Amazon, etc.).
- `transparent/` — cut-out masters (WebP with alpha) for new backgrounds, banners or lookbooks.

## Specs

- 2048 × 2048 px square (Shopify recommendation), sRGB JPEG, quality 90, ~300 KB each.
- Product fills ~72–74 % of the frame, centred; same framing across the set.
- Background of the original shoot was neutralised (it had a cool blue cast), so colours
  read slightly warmer and truer than the raw photos.

## Benchmark

Background and framing follow what luxury leather houses use: toned warm/neutral greys rather
than pure white (Hermès ~#E3E0D9, Connolly #E1E0DC, Métier #E6E6E6, Valextra #F6F6F6),
soft contact shadows, no reflections, square 1:1 crops, one consistent look per catalogue.
