# PA & CO — client brief

Italian luxury leather-goods brand (client of the repo owner). Logo SVGs in `/brand/`
("Limited Edition" line removed; horizontal lockup used in web headers).

## Product facts (use exactly, facts only)

- Calf leather, embossed with a crocodile pattern (watch case: plain pebbled calf — confirm before
  calling it croc)
- Lining: 100% Alcantara
- Hardware: solid brass, palladium-plated
- Handmade in Italy

## Image rules agreed with the owner

- Main image: straight front view, except both backpacks lead with a matching **3/4 view**
  (same direction for both). Front view is then `02`.
- Backpack colours share one recoloured photo for the main + front images (identical angle).
  Mini backpack uses real photos per colour (recolour could not reproduce the black gloss).
- Mini backpack full-bag shots framed at ~54 % (others 72 %) so it reads smaller than the Backpack.
- One zip close-up per product; no duplicate detail shots.
- Stone `#EDEAE4` everywhere; white-background versions also delivered.
- Source photos: first shoot IMG_4481–4785 (1920 px), reshoot IMG_9082–9242 (24 MP, preferred).

## Website mockup direction

Luxury, calm, mobile-first: warm off-white page, stone image tiles, Cormorant Garamond headings +
Jost body, thin rules, no badges. Mobile: burger left, logo centred, bag right; 2-column grid;
swipe gallery with counter + thumbnails; accordions (Description / Materials / Care); sticky
add-to-bag bar. No "Handmade in Italy" top banner. Prices, dimensions and delivery terms still to
be supplied by the client.
