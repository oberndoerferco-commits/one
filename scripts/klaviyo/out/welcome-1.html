We make trunks, bags and objects for the home by hand, in small ateliers around Milan, to order and in small numbers. Nothing is cut until it is yours.

The emblem, cut in solid brass and brazed by hand.

The people who make the pieces learned from their parents and grandparents. The workshops are fewer every year. We stand behind them, stud by stud, so that what lies behind the words Made in Italy does not disappear.

This letter arrives once a month, no more: a new piece, a commission in progress, a photograph from the bench. You hear of new pieces and pre-orders here first, before the site. Over the next ten days, two short notes follow: one on the trunks, one on the pieces you can order today.
