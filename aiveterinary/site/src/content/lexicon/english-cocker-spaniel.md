## How long do English Cocker Spaniels live?

Longer than most dogs. A 2024 UK study of 26,303 English Cocker Spaniels found a median lifespan of 13.3 years, against 12.0 years for crossbred dogs (McMillan et al.). VetCompass life tables, built from UK vet records, gave 1,073 Cocker Spaniels a life expectancy at birth of 11.31 years, close to the 11.23 years for dogs overall (Teng et al.). The Royal Kennel Club gives the breed's lifespan as over 10 years.

## How big does a Cocker Spaniel get?

Small to medium. The FCI breed standard gives a height of about 39 to 41 cm for males and 38 to 39 cm for females, and a weight of about 13 to 14.5 kg. In the UK the Royal Kennel Club lists the breed as "Spaniel (Cocker)"; the American Cocker Spaniel is a separate breed.

## What health problems do Cocker Spaniels have?

**Ears and eyes:** a VetCompass study compared 2,510 English Cocker Spaniels with 7,813 other dogs in UK vet records. Cockers had higher odds for 21 of the 43 common disorders studied, the highest being ear discharge (14.66 times the odds) and keratoconjunctivitis sicca, or dry eye (7.64 times). The authors call ear and eye problems key health issues for the breed. Check the long ears every week; see [ear infections in dogs](/animals/dog-ear-infection). Red, sticky or squinting eyes need a vet.

**Fewer allergies:** the same study found lower odds than other dogs of atopic dermatitis and allergy (both 0.14 times the odds).

**Kidneys:** the UFAW genetic welfare database describes familial nephropathy, an inherited kidney disease in which young dogs, usually between 6 months and 2 years old, develop kidney failure. Signs include drinking and urinating a lot, poor appetite, tiredness, vomiting and bad breath. It is fatal. It is inherited as a recessive trait, and UFAW says careful breeding programmes have reduced the number of dogs affected; the Royal Kennel Club lists a DNA test for it.

**Other conditions** listed by the PDSA include hip dysplasia, skin allergies, some auto-immune diseases, adult-onset neuropathy and acral mutilation syndrome, a painful nerve disease of the paws.

## Which health tests matter for a Cocker Spaniel puppy?

The Royal Kennel Club lists as good practice before breeding DNA tests for acral mutilation syndrome and for progressive retinal atrophy (prcd-PRA), an inherited loss of sight. As best practice it adds DNA tests for adult-onset neuropathy and familial nephropathy, eye testing with gonioscopy (a check of the drainage angle inside the eye) under the BVA/KC/ISDS Eye Scheme, and hip scoring. Ask to see both parents' results.

## Is a Cocker Spaniel right for me?

The FCI standard describes a merry dog with an ever-wagging tail, gentle and affectionate, yet full of life and exuberance. That only tells you what breeders aim for. The Royal Kennel Club gives up to 1 hour of exercise a day and daily grooming; the coat sheds. The PDSA says Cockers are people-oriented, like company all day, and may chew or bark when bored.
