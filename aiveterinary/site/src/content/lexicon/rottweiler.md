## How long do Rottweilers live?

Shorter than most dogs. A 2024 UK study of 9,717 Rottweilers found a median lifespan of 10.6 years, against 12.0 years for crossbred dogs (McMillan et al.). An earlier VetCompass study of UK vet records found a median of 9.0 years across 415 deaths, with females living longer than males (9.5 against 8.7 years) (O'Neill et al., 2017). The Royal Kennel Club gives the breed's lifespan as under 10 years.

## How big does a Rottweiler get?

Large and powerful. The FCI breed standard gives a height at the withers of 61 to 68 cm for males, with 65 to 66 cm called correct, and 56 to 63 cm for females, with 60 to 61 cm correct; males weigh 50 kg and females about 42 kg. In UK vet records the median adult bodyweight was 48.5 kg for males and 41.5 kg for females (O'Neill et al.).

## What health problems do Rottweilers have?

**Cancer:** in the VetCompass study, neoplasia caused 33.0% of deaths with a known cause, and some deaths put down to "masses" may also have been cancer. The UFAW genetic welfare database says Rottweilers are predisposed to osteosarcoma, an aggressive bone cancer: an estimated 5 to 12% of the breed are affected, at an average age of about 8 years. It usually starts in a front leg and causes severe pain and lameness; by the time it is found it has usually spread, and treatment succeeds in only about 10% of cases. A swollen or painful leg in an older Rottweiler needs a vet promptly: see [limping in dogs](/animals/dog-limping).

**Common problems:** the most frequent diagnoses in VetCompass records were aggression (7.46%), overweight or obesity (7.06%), ear infection (6.14%) and degenerative joint disease (4.69%). Aggression was recorded more often in males (9.36%) than females (5.47%).

**Other conditions** listed by the PDSA include subaortic stenosis (a narrowing below a heart valve that can cause a murmur, fainting and sudden death), cruciate ligament damage, hip and elbow dysplasia, osteochondrosis of the hock, shoulder or knee, juvenile laryngeal paralysis and polyneuropathy, inherited eye diseases, lymphoma and hot spots on the skin.

## Which health tests matter for a Rottweiler puppy?

The Royal Kennel Club lists as good practice before breeding a DNA test for juvenile laryngeal paralysis and polyneuropathy (JLPP) and hip and elbow scoring under the BVA/KC schemes. As best practice it adds a DNA test for leukoencephalomyelopathy (LEMP-2) and eye testing. The breed is in Breed Watch category 2, which means particular points of concern have been identified for the breed. Ask to see both parents' results; the PDSA also advises heart screening of breeding dogs.

## Is a Rottweiler right for me?

The FCI standard describes a good-natured, placid, very devoted, obedient and biddable dog, eager to work, self-assured, steady and fearless, reacting to its surroundings with great alertness while staying even-tempered. That only tells you what breeders aim for. The Royal Kennel Club says the breed needs responsible owners who can channel its intelligence and activity, and gives more than 2 hours of exercise a day and a large garden. The PDSA stresses early socialisation and consistent reward-based training, and says Rottweilers should not be left unsupervised with children.
