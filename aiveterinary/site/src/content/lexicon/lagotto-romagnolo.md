## How long do Lagotti live?

The Royal Kennel Club gives the breed's lifespan as over 12 years. We found no large published study of the Lagotto's lifespan, so treat any exact figure you see with caution.

## How big does a Lagotto Romagnolo get?

Small to medium. The FCI breed standard describes a well-proportioned, powerfully built dog of rustic appearance with a square body. Males stand 43 to 48 cm at the withers and weigh 13 to 16 kg; females stand 41 to 46 cm and weigh 11 to 14 kg.

## How do I care for a Lagotto's coat?

The coat is the breed's trademark: woolly, slightly rough on the surface, with tight ring-shaped curls and a visible undercoat, and waterproof. The FCI standard warns that if it is not cared for the hair keeps growing and felts, and the felted coat must be removed from time to time. It asks for a simple clip no longer than 2 to 3 cm that follows the dog's outline, with the eyes not covered by hair and the area around the genitals and anus clipped short. The Royal Kennel Club lists grooming more than once a week and says the coat does not shed.

## What is a Lagotto like?

The Lagotto was once a water retriever in the marshes of Comacchio and Ravenna and became a truffle dog when the marshes were drained. The FCI standard says its natural gift for searching and very good nose make it efficient at finding truffles, and that its old hunting instinct has been reduced by selection so it is not distracted by game. It describes a tractable, undemanding, keen and affectionate dog, very attached to its owner, easy to train, a very good companion and an excellent watchdog. That is the breeding goal, not a promise for every dog.

## How much exercise does a Lagotto need?

More than you might expect for its size. The Royal Kennel Club gives more than 2 hours a day and a large garden, and says the breed suits the country best.

## What health problems do Lagotti have?

**Juvenile epilepsy:** some Lagotto puppies have seizures from around 7 weeks of age that stop by themselves by about 4 months. Researchers in Finland traced this benign juvenile epilepsy to a variant of the LGI2 gene. In 576 Lagotti from three countries, 32% carried the variant, so a DNA test matters before breeding (Seppälä et al.). Any seizure in a puppy needs a vet.

**Storage disease:** a different, inherited brain disease causes progressive cerebellar ataxia, a worsening loss of coordination, sometimes with flickering eye movements and behaviour changes. In 22 affected Lagotti, the first signs appeared at a mean age of 23 months, ranging from 4 months to 4 years. It is linked to a change in the ATG4D gene (Kyöstilä et al.). The Royal Kennel Club lists a DNA test for lysosomal storage disease (LSD) for the breed.

**Joints and eyes:** the Royal Kennel Club also recommends hip and elbow scoring and eye testing before breeding. For lameness see [limping in dogs](/animals/dog-limping).

## Which health tests matter for a Lagotto puppy?

The Royal Kennel Club lists DNA tests for juvenile epilepsy (JE) and lysosomal storage disease (LSD) as good practice before breeding, and hip scoring, elbow scoring and eye testing under the BVA/KC schemes as best practice. Ask to see both parents' results.

## Is a Lagotto right for me?

Plan for an active, nose-driven working dog that needs over two hours of exercise and something to search for every day, and a coat that needs brushing more than once a week and regular clipping. Ask for the parents' DNA, hip, elbow and eye results.
