# Germany: pet registries, HABS, federal plans and EU Regulation 2026/1818

As of 23 October 2026. This note answers the open TODO item "DE: HABS status between TASSO and FINDEFIX". It also checks the Germany rows on the registry pages (`site/src/pages/registry.astro`, `it/registry.astro`, `de/registry.astro`) against primary sources. All sources below were opened on 2026-10-23 unless marked otherwise.

## Short answers

| Question | Answer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| What is HABS, and does it work now? | HABS is the **Heimtierabfrageservice**: a planned digital interface that would link the existing German pet registries so a chip number can be looked up across all of them, later through a HABS website. The association **Heimtierverantwortung e.V.** (Dortmund, 6 members, register no. R007015) is to "prepare, found and run" it. Its lobby register entry, last updated 1 Aug 2026, still describes HABS in the future tense ("sollen … verbunden werden", "könnten zukünftig … erfolgen"). I found no public HABS site and no mention of HABS on tasso.net or findefix.com. **Not a working service.** | High that it is a plan, not live (official register text, Aug 2026). Medium on whether anything changed between Aug and Oct 2026: htva.de returned 503. |
| Are TASSO and FINDEFIX connected (mutual lookup)? Via Europetnet? | **No joint lookup found.** **TASSO** is a full Europetnet member (Eschborn). **FINDEFIX / Deutscher Tierschutzbund is not** on the Europetnet member or associate list. TASSO's FAQ says it is connected to the meta-search engines **europetnet.com and petmaxx.com**, which return only *which* registry holds the number, never the owner's data. **petmaxx.com** lists both "Germany: Tasso" and "Germany: Deutscher Tierschutzbund" among the databases it searches, so petmaxx is the one place that searches both. FINDEFIX's FAQ suggests registering in both for safety and says nothing of a mutual lookup. The Niedersachsen register's FAQ says it does **not** match its data with TASSO, and Berlin's says a private registry cannot hand its data to the state register. | High (member list, both FAQs, petmaxx list). |
| Is a federal pet registry or chip-duty law adopted or in progress in 2026? | **No.** The 2024 bill (BT-Drs. 20/12719, BR-Drs. 256/24) of the 20th Bundestag amended § 2a(1b) TierSchG on registration. It is still cited in lobby entries. Its status on DIP was not checked, but TASSO's 2026 page says that "bislang" there is no nationwide duty, so it did not create one. The 2025 coalition agreement (CDU/CSU–SPD) bans anonymous online sales and the sale of pets in public places but has **no** chip or registration duty, and the Deutscher Tierschutzbund's 9 Apr 2025 comment complains about exactly that. The only TierSchG bill of the 21st term on the BMLEH site, the *Fünftes Gesetz zur Änderung des Tierschutzgesetzes* (draft of 3 Feb 2026, cabinet version published 5 May 2026), is about **video surveillance in slaughterhouses**. Neither version mentions dogs, cats, chips or registers. TASSO's page (after the EU vote) says it is still undecided how Germany will implement the EU rules. | High for "nothing adopted" and "no BMLEH bill". Medium for "nothing anywhere in the Bundestag": DIP could not be searched (see below). |
| Has the EU dog/cat welfare and traceability regulation been adopted, and what does it require? | **Yes: Regulation (EU) 2026/1818** of 17 June 2026, OJ L of 10.8.2026, in force since **30 Aug 2026** (20th day after publication; Art. 28(2) confirms "from 30 August 2026"). EP vote 28 Apr 2026, Council 22 May 2026. **Art. 20**: every dog and cat gets a single injectable ISO microchip (Annex II), implanted by a vet unless the Member State allows trained others. Breeders chip within 3 months of birth, shelters and shops within 30 days of arrival, owners by the age of 3 months or before sale. The **vet registers the animal in a national database within 2 working days** (Member States may let others do it). A change of owner after an occasional private sale or gift must be recorded **within 2 weeks**, and a death must be recorded. Chips implanted before 31 Aug 2028 count if still readable. **Art. 20(7) timelines**: from **31 Aug 2030** for anyone placing dogs or cats on the market, **31 Aug 2036** for private dog owners and **31 Aug 2041** for private cat owners. **Art. 23**: each Member State sets up and runs database(s), which must be **interoperable**; the Commission runs an **index database**. Implementing acts on content and proof are due by 31 Aug 2028, on interoperability by 31 Aug 2029. Art. 23(1) applies from 31 Aug 2030 and Art. 23(3)–(4) from 31 Aug 2031. **Art. 30**: stricter national rules are allowed. The rest of the Regulation applies from 31 Aug 2028. | High (OJ text read). |
| Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/132 (cited on the site and in docs/21) | It amends Delegated Reg. 2019/2035 for **dogs, cats and ferrets moved to another Member State**. They need an approved transponder implanted by an official or authorised vet, and the passport model is now that of Impl. Reg. 2026/705. From **1 Jan 2028** the chip code must start with the ISO 3166 code of the country of first identification. It applies from 22 Apr 2026. **It contains no registry or database duty**, so it does not change the field lists in docs/21. | High (OJ text read). |
| Berlin and Niedersachsen: is the site right? | Mostly yes. Fees are confirmed (Berlin €17.50 online / €26.50 by form or phone; Niedersachsen €14.50 / €23.50), as are Berlin's chip duty from 3 months and the tax link (registration counts as dog-tax registration since 1 Jan 2024, deregistration since 8 Nov 2024). **What was wrong or missing:** (1) The site said the registry sites do not state a deadline. They do. Berlin: registration **when keeping starts** (§ 13(1) HundeG per the register FAQ); a puppy under 3 months is registered at once and the chip number added later. Niedersachsen: **§ 6(1) NHundG**, before the dog reaches **7 months**, or **within 1 month** if it is over 6 months when keeping starts. (2) Niedersachsen applies to dogs **over six months** (§ 4 chip, § 6 registration), not "all dogs". (3) **§ 6(2) NHundG**: giving up the dog, its loss or death, and a new address must be reported within **1 month**. (4) Change of owner, in both Länder: the old keeper deregisters and the new keeper registers and pays again. (5) Niedersachsen fine up to €10,000 (§ 18 NHundG, per FAQ). | High for Niedersachsen (statute read). Medium-high for Berlin (register FAQ citing HundeG; the statute itself could not be opened). |
| Cats | The site said "Cats: never mandatory". TASSO's registration-duty page says outdoor cats must be chipped and registered in many places under local **Katzenschutzverordnungen** (often with neutering). Corrected. | Medium-high (TASSO page; individual ordinances not opened). |

## What changed on the site

All three languages, Germany section only:

- **Registry**: added that there is no joint lookup yet, that the Land registers are separate (Niedersachsen does not match its data with TASSO), and that HABS is a plan, not a service.
- **Mandatory for**: Niedersachsen is now "dogs older than six months". Cats now mention local cat-protection ordinances. Added EU Reg. 2026/1818 with its 2030/2036/2041 dates and "no federal bill yet".
- **Deadline after acquisition**: Berlin "at start of keeping" and Niedersachsen § 6(1) NHundG replace "ask the Ordnungsamt". Added the change-of-owner procedure.
- **Loss/death**: added Niedersachsen's 1-month rule (§ 6(2)) and Berlin's duties with no stated deadline. Changes are free in both.
- **Moving to Germany**: the deadline text now matches.
- **Across borders**: FINDEFIX is not in Europetnet; petmaxx searches both German registries.
- **Sources**: added the Berlin and Niedersachsen FAQs, NHundG §§ 4 and 6, TASSO's registration-duty page, lobby register R007015 and the Regulation 2026/1818 OJ copy, plus petmaxx.

Not changed: phone numbers, fees, "Who registers", and "What you need". The site says Land registers ask for "ID data". The § 6 NHundG list is name, date and place of birth, address, and the dog's sex, birth date, breed and chip number, so "ID data" is loose but not wrong enough to change without Berlin's § 11 text.

## Could not open, or not found

- **htva.de** (Heimtierverantwortung e.V.) returned 503, and **habs.de** failed the TLS handshake. Neither the HABS site nor the association's own news could be read, so anything after the 1 Aug 2026 lobby register update is unverified. The member list is not public in the register (3 natural persons, 3 organisations); whether TASSO or FINDEFIX are members is **unknown**. An undated DocCheck article found by search says TASSO took part in a working group that built HABS, but the page body did not load.
- **heimtierverantwortung.de**: refused by the proxy.
- **gesetze.berlin.de** (Berlin HundeG): connection reset on every URL, and WebFetch got 503. The Berlin statements rely on the register's FAQ, which cites §§ 11–13 and 34 HundeG. The FAQ cites the chip duty as both § 12 and § 4 HundeG, so the page gives no paragraph number.
- **DIP (dip.bundestag.de)**: the API needs a key, and the search UI is JavaScript-only. I could not confirm on DIP that no 21st-term Drucksache on pet registration exists. The negative finding rests on BMLEH's draft-law page, the coalition agreement and TASSO's statement.
- **EUR-Lex HTML** for 2026/1818 returned 202 with an empty body. The identical OJ text came from the Publications Office cellar.
- **nds-voris.de jportal** link: 404. The current NI-VORIS host is voris.wolterskluwer-online.de.
- Individual **Katzenschutzverordnungen** were not opened. The cat statement rests on TASSO's page.
