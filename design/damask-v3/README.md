# Damask v3 — refined

The v2 "Grand logo" layout, redrawn for elegance:

- Calligraphic strokes: curls swell in the middle, thin at the ends and finish in a teardrop.
- Clear weight order: ornaments boldest; frame lighter and tapered to fine points.
- Paisleys fill the ovals; about half the loose dots removed.
- Calmer clusters: one bead spine, two small flowers, one pair of ram's-horn scrolls, two leaves.

Two logo treatments:

- `damask-v3-solid-tile.svg`: logo raised as one solid shape, like the flower in the photo.
- `damask-v3-outline-tile.svg`: logo as a line with a smaller echo inside, like the mark itself.

Both are one 150 × 150 mm repeat, black artwork at true size; logo 61.5 mm.
The thinnest stroke is 1.05 mm. `damask-v3-compare.jpg` puts both, embossed
and as artwork, next to the reference photo.

Regenerate with `node design/damask-v3/generate.js`; `node design/damask-v3/preview.js`
writes the embossed preview pages. Built on the shapes in `../damask-v2/generate.js`.
