# Milan Packaging Register

Packaging producers near Milan, in the mould of G.Box (gboxsrl.it), for
Oberndörfer Milano.

**Scope:** 69 packaging houses across five trades.
**Interactive version:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/8cda6450-b04d-4d5e-9f73-4ae00c6097a9
(filterable, with spec tags per row)

## How to read the status field

- **Verified** — the company's own site or a confirmed company profile was read;
  the description is theirs. 22 of 69.
- **Register lead** — the firm appears in a chamber-of-commerce or packaging
  trade directory under that trade at that address, but the fit is unchecked.
  47 of 69. **Do not present a lead as a recommendation.**

Spec tags — founding year, processes, finishes, certifications, minimums —
are recorded only where the company publishes them.

## The reference point

G.Box, the maker the founder sent as the benchmark, is in Cornate d'Adda (MB),
founded 1991, ~2,000 m², 6–9 staff, ~€2M revenue. It makes boxes, couvettes and
precious caskets for perfume, cosmetics, fashion and champagne, and has been in
a joint venture with Giovanardi Spa since 2011.

The closest equivalents found: **Kartolab** (Cusano Milanino), **L'Ottagono**
(Biassono), **Montanelli 3** (Burago di Molgora) and **Faenza Couture** (Rodano).

**Faenza Couture is the only house in the register that publishes a minimum:**
1 to 1,000 pieces at the same quality, with same-day prototypes. That single
fact will probably decide the supplier choice.

Stilema/Stilux has a Milan showroom but manufactures in the Far East — flagged
here, not a made-in-Italy option.
