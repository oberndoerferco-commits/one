# Lombardy Maker Register

Leather-goods makers in and around Milan — bags, trunks and astucci — for
Oberndörfer Milano.

**Scope:** 67 makers plus 6 supply houses across MI, MB, LC, VA, CO and PV.
**Interactive version:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0ac76cec-d250-44a8-b5aa-327071d2699c
(filterable by craft, verification level and name/town)

## How to read the status field

- **Verified** — the company's own site or a confirmed company profile was read;
  the description is theirs. 25 of the 67 makers.
- **Register lead** — the firm appears in a chamber-of-commerce or trade
  directory under that trade at that address, but the fit is unchecked. Some
  will turn out to be repairers or retailers. 42 of the 67 makers.
  **Do not present a lead as a recommendation.**

The 6 supply houses in section IV carry the same labels (2 verified, 4 leads).

## Geography

Brianza — Seregno, Giussano, Lissone, Limbiate — holds the bag-workshop
density. Milan itself holds the trunk and case makers. Vigevano was the world's
shoe capital but its finished-goods makers have thinned; its real value now is
ISEM, which makes luxury rigid boxes for Coty, L'Oréal, Shiseido and LVMH.

Tuscany and the Marche are deliberately excluded. That is where the depth of
contract leather work and the low-minimum private-label houses actually are,
but nothing from there supports "Handmade in Milan."
