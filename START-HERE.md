# OBERNDÖRFER logo — start here

Branch `claude/logo-ribbon`. Read this, then
`assets/oberndorfer-plate/DIRECTION.md`. Those two outrank anything
you infer from the repo or from an earlier conversation.

## The job

A two-tier mark: OBERNDÖRFER on a bent ribbon, MADE IN ITALY on its
own smaller panel below, the two overlapping so they read as one
object.

## Where it stands

Open `assets/oberndorfer-plate/canva-fit/all.png`. Four typefaces set
on four ribbons, all taken from the owner's own Canva file, each
wordmark bent to its ribbon's real curve. A ribbon and a face need
choosing; then MADE IN ITALY goes on its panel and the whole thing is
cleaned up.

The engraved and the gold faces read strongest. Best so far:
r04_2-engraved, r08_2-engraved, r04_3-gold.

## Rules, learned the hard way over a long session

1. ONE change per note, shown. Never a sheet of fifty variants.
2. LOOK at every image before sending it. Several were sent unchecked
   and were unusable.
3. The owner's own references and assets beat anything drawn from
   scratch. Use them first, always.
4. Measure to CHECK a design at the end. Never to choose one. Letting
   manufacturing limits drive the aesthetics wasted most of a session.
5. The owner will contradict earlier instructions. That is fine. The
   latest wins: record it in DIRECTION.md and strike the old one into
   Superseded.
