# Shipping policy – text to paste

Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Shipping policy. Replace everything in the box with the text below.

Regions of Delivery
We ship worldwide with DHL, including the USA, Canada, Europe and Asia.

Shipping Costs
Shipping is free on every order from €300, worldwide.

Below €300, the shipping cost depends on the pieces in your order:
- T-shirts: €15 within the EU, €50 outside the EU
- Hoodies, half-zip and beanie: €20 within the EU, €55 outside the EU
- Trousers and joggers: €20 within the EU, €50 outside the EU
- Belts: €25 within the EU, €69 outside the EU
- Caps: €29, everywhere
- Trays, coasters and crochet bags: €29 within the EU, €75 outside the EU

If your order contains pieces from more than one of these groups, their shipping costs are added together. The exact amount is shown at checkout before payment. Furniture and very large pieces are delivered by arrangement after your order.

Personalisation
Initials cost €60 per piece and are added on the product page before checkout.

Delivery Times
Pieces in stock are delivered within 7–14 days of your order. You will receive tracking details as soon as your order ships.

Made to Order
Our larger commissions - trunks, sofas and furniture - are built to order by hand in our ateliers around Milan. Trunks take 2–6 months depending on complexity and level of personalisation; furniture and highly detailed commissions take 4–6 months. We confirm your piece's timeline right after your order, or in advance if you contact us.

Customs and Duties
Within the EU, prices include VAT. Customers outside the EU are responsible for any customs fees or import taxes that may apply in their country.

Contact Us
For shipping enquiries, email us at info@oberndoerferco.com.
