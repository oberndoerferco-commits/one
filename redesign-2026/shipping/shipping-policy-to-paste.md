Thank you for shopping with Oberndörfer Milano. This policy explains how we ship your order, what it costs, and what to expect after you buy.

Shipping Costs
Shipping is free on every order from €300, worldwide.

Below €300, the shipping cost depends on the pieces in your order:
- T-shirts: €15 within the EU, €50 outside the EU
- Hoodies, half-zip and beanie: €20 within the EU, €55 outside the EU
- Trousers and joggers: €20 within the EU, €50 outside the EU
- Belts: €25 within the EU, €69 outside the EU
- Caps: €29, everywhere
- Trays, coasters and crochet bags: €29 within the EU, €75 outside the EU

If your order contains pieces from more than one of these groups, their shipping costs are added together. The exact amount is shown at checkout before you pay. Furniture and very large pieces are delivered by arrangement after your order.

Order Processing
Pieces in stock are processed within 4 business days, excluding weekends and holidays. Orders placed after noon, or on weekends and holidays, begin processing the next business day. Made-to-order pieces follow the timeline below.

Delivery Times
We ship with DHL. Pieces in stock usually arrive within 7–14 days of your order. Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees, and may be affected by carrier delays, customs, weather or other events outside our control.

Made to Order
Our larger commissions - trunks, sofas and furniture - are built to order by hand in our ateliers around Milan. Trunks take 2–6 months depending on complexity and level of personalisation; furniture and highly detailed commissions take 4–6 months. We confirm your piece's timeline right after your order, or in advance if you contact us.

Personalisation
Initials cost €60 per piece and are added on the product page before checkout.

Order Tracking
Once your order ships, we email you a confirmation with a tracking number and link. Tracking information may take a little time to update.

Shipping Destinations
We ship worldwide. The countries we deliver to are shown at checkout.

Customs, Duties and Taxes
Within the European Union: prices include VAT and your order ships from within the EU, so there are no customs duties or import fees.

Outside the European Union (including the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States, Canada and Japan): your order may be subject to import duties, taxes such as VAT or GST, and carrier brokerage or handling fees. Where these are calculated at checkout, they are shown before you pay and are not due at delivery. Where they are not collected at checkout, they may be due before or at delivery and are the customer's responsibility. Customs processing may also delay delivery. We recommend checking your local import rules before ordering.

Address Accuracy
Please check your shipping address at checkout. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to correct it.

Lost, Damaged or Delayed Packages
If your order is lost, damaged or delayed, contact us and we will help you resolve it with DHL.

Order Changes and Cancellations
Need to change or cancel your order? Contact us as soon as possible. We can usually make changes before your order ships; once it is in transit we cannot modify it. Refunds for cancelled orders are made to the original payment method. Personalised and made-to-order pieces are covered by our Returns & Cancellations policy.
