Shopify admin → Settings → Policies → Shipping policy. (Claude has no permission to edit legal policies.)

Regions of Delivery
We ship worldwide - including the USA, Canada, Europe, Asia, and other international destinations - with DHL.

Shipping Costs
DHL Standard is free on all orders, worldwide. DHL Express is free on orders of €300 or more. On orders below €300, DHL Express costs €5 for small pieces and €20 for larger pieces within the European Union, and €50 for small pieces and €65 for larger pieces outside the European Union. The applicable price is shown at checkout before payment. For furniture and very large pieces, delivery is arranged with the customer after the order.

Personalisation
Personalisation with initials costs €60 per piece and is added on the product page before checkout.

Delivery Times
Every piece on the website is delivered within 7-14 days of your order. You will receive tracking details as soon as your order ships.

Made to Order
Our larger commissions - trunks, sofas, and furniture - are built to order by hand in our ateliers around Milan. Trunks take 2–6 months depending on complexity and level of personalization; furniture and highly detailed commissions take 4–6 months. We confirm your piece's timeline right after your order, or in advance if you contact us.

Customs and Duties
International customers are responsible for any customs fees or import taxes that may apply in their country.

For shipping inquiries, email us at info@oberndoerferco.com.
