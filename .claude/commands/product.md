Bring a product page up to house standard: **$ARGUMENTS**

Use the `merchandiser` agent.

1. Fetch the product — current title, description, tags, metadata, variants,
   inventory quantity and policy, and images.
2. Check whether it can actually be bought. Inventory 0 with `DENY` policy is a
   dead end; report it before anything else.
3. Rewrite the description to carry, in this order:
   - the material, named and specific — which hide, tanned how
   - the hand that made it, where, and roughly how long it took
   - **dimensions and weight** (the most requested missing fact)
   - free DHL Express worldwide, 7–14 days, stated on the page
   - scarcity as preference, where inventory is genuinely 1
   - a link to leather care
4. Write a meta title and description for a human reading a search result,
   mining the existing product tags for the phrase people actually type.
5. Strip every retire-list word.

Show me the before and after side by side. **Do not write it to the store until
I say go.** Never change the price.
