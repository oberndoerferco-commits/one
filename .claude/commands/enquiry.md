Handle this enquiry: **$ARGUMENTS**

Use the `concierge` agent.

1. Identify which piece or pieces the enquiry concerns, and pull the real
   details — material, dimensions, price, availability, CITES status if exotic.
2. Work out what the person is actually asking. Often the stated question
   ("is it in stock?") is not the real one ("is this house real, and will my
   money be safe?").
3. Draft a reply in the house register: warm, unhurried, specific. Named
   material, named hand, real dates. No urgency, no discount, no pressure.
4. Where it helps, offer what a shop cannot — photographs of the actual piece,
   a video of it in the light, a call, a visit to the atelier, or a commission
   in their own choice of hide.
5. Note anything we should send with it: the dossier, CITES documentation,
   care instructions.

Give me the draft ready to send. **I send it, not you.** Then log the enquiry
and the piece to `docs/handbook/enquiries.md`.
