Handle this enquiry: **$ARGUMENTS**

`client-advisor` leads.

1. Identify the piece or pieces, and pull the real details — material,
   dimensions, price, availability, CITES status if exotic. `atelier-director`
   supplies anything about the making.
2. Work out what is actually being asked. "Is it in stock?" usually means *is
   this house real, and is my money safe?*
3. Draft the reply in house register: warm, unhurried, specific. Named material,
   named hand, real dates. No urgency, no discount, no pressure.
4. Offer what a shop cannot — photographs of the actual piece, film of it in
   the light, a call, a visit to the atelier, or a commission in their own hide.
5. Note what should travel with it: the dossier, CITES paperwork, care.

`creative-director` checks the register.

Give me the draft ready to send. **I send it, not the house.** Then log the
enquiry and the piece to `docs/handbook/enquiries.md`.
