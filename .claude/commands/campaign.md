Brief a campaign: **$ARGUMENTS**

`creative-director` leads and sets the direction first — what the house is
saying, which pieces carry it, and what it should look like. Everything below
follows that, not the other way round.

Then, in parallel:

- `merchandising-manager` — which pieces, why those, and whether their pages
  are ready to receive traffic. A campaign pointing at a weak page wastes itself.
- `atelier-director` — the material facts the campaign can stand on. Named hide,
  named tannage, real hours.
- `art-director` — the imagery and any film. **Real photographs of real pieces**;
  generation only for room, light and atmosphere.
- `editorial-director` — the words, and a journal piece if the subject earns one.
- `communications` — where it goes, at what cadence, and any partner worth
  approaching for it.

`creative-director` approves the whole thing as one piece of work before
anything ships. Then show me: the direction in two sentences, the pieces, the
assets, the words, and the schedule.

**Nothing publishes until I approve it.** No urgency language, no discounting.
