# Site audit, 23 September 2026 (first run)

**Summary.** The site works and every page loads, but a looping script was making
every product page sluggish under the finger. Two real issues found, both fixed on the
"NEW WEBSITE BUG fix 13 - touch responsiveness" copy and waiting for you to publish.
Fix first: publish fix 13.

Run: 40 page loads (20 pages, desktop and iPhone emulation), 10 product pages, 157 internal
links, the catalogue feed and the email DNS, 137 seconds. Raw findings in 2026-09-23.json.

## High

1. **Pages keep changing while idle** (home, Bags, Trunks, Home Accessories, Travel, search
   results; desktop and mobile). 735 to 2,592 DOM nodes replaced every 3 seconds on a page
   nobody is touching. Confirmed by the same measurement on the live site and on the fix 13
   preview (56 on the home page, 0 elsewhere). Cause: the colour-dot script rebuilt its own
   rows eight times a second. This is the "first tap does nothing" bug you reported. Fixed on
   fix 13. Size: done; publish.

## Medium

2. **Script error on every page with several sections** ("Identifier 'section' has already
   been declared", four times on the home page, once on About us and Bespoke). Cause: a stock
   Horizon 4.2.0 snippet declares a top-level constant in an inline script, so the second
   and later copies on a page fail. Effect is small (the header height measurement only needs
   the first copy) but it is a real error in every visitor's console and can mask others.
   Fixed on fix 13 by scoping the declaration; confirmed 0 errors on the preview. Size: done.

## Low

3. **Account link answers 406 to a HEAD request** (`/customer_authentication/redirect`).
   Shopify's own endpoint; it works in a browser. Not a fault. The audit now skips it.

## Not faults, fixed in the audit itself

- `/pages/leather-care` answered 404: the page's real address is `/pages/leather-care-guide`
  and nothing on the site links to the wrong one. The audit's page list was wrong.
- The deliberately missing page probe reported its own 404 as a failed request. Silenced.

## Checked and fine

All 20 pages answer 200 (the missing-page probe answers 404 as it should). No broken
images on any page or product page sampled. No sideways scroll on mobile. First tap on a
product card opens the product on every collection page in the emulation. Every product
page sampled has a title, price, photographs and a buy or pre-order button. Catalogue feed:
no active product without a photograph, with an empty description or at 0. Email DNS:
DMARC, Shopify DKIM and Zoho MX present. No failed requests apart from the ones above.

## Deliberate choices seen and not reported

Pre-order buttons and notes on Ready to Wear; one card per colour on Ready to Wear; colour
dots under same-model leather cards; the footer monogram; 12px card swatches.
