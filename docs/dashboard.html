The one decision that is only yours

Name the leather.

Poltrona Frau has Pelle Frau®. Serapian has Mosaico. An unnamed hide is a commodity; a named one is proprietary — and it changes every product page in the catalogue at once.

The copy review identified it as the highest-leverage change available to the whole house. atelier-director can establish what the leather actually is — the tanneries, the tannage, what makes it distinguishable — and bring you a short list of defensible names. The choice is yours.

Everyone inherits CLAUDE.md — the maison register, the retire list, and eight guardrails. Two never bend: anything the public will see passes creative-director, and any claim about a material passes atelier-director. Nothing touches the live theme. No price changes, no deletions, no posting, no sending, no spending without you saying so.
