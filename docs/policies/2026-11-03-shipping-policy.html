Regions of Delivery
We ship worldwide - including the USA, Canada, Europe, Asia, and other international destinations, with DHL.

Shipping Costs
DHL Standard is free on every order, worldwide. DHL Express is free from €300. Below €300, DHL Express costs €5 for small pieces and €20 for larger pieces within the EU, and €50 or €65 outside the EU. Furniture is delivered by arrangement after your order.

Delivery Times
Pieces in stock arrive in 7–14 days with DHL Standard and in 2–5 days with DHL Express. You will receive tracking details as soon as your order ships.

Made to Order
Our larger commissions - trunks, sofas, and furniture - are built to order by hand in our ateliers around Milan. Trunks take 2–6 months depending on complexity and level of personalization; furniture and highly detailed commissions take 4–6 months. We confirm your piece's timeline right after your order, or in advance if you contact us.

Customs and Duties
Within the EU, prices include VAT. International customers outside the EU are responsible for any customs fees or import taxes that may apply in their country.
