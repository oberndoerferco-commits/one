Rules the grid follows

  • Three Reels, six carousels. Reels reach strangers; carousels out-view Reels for followers and earn saves.
  • Nothing on a white sweep. Your white-background product shots are the lowest posts on the account at 500 to 650 views.
  • Dark, warm, dark. Each row alternates a bench frame, a warm interior, a quiet surface, so the grid reads as one room.
  • Every tile is a product the store sells. Watch boxes and trays only. The vanity cases and trunks return when the store carries them.
  • One fact per post. Stud count, leather type, cutting order. Specific beats "passion and tradition".
  • No prices in the image, no urgency, five hashtags at most. Prices live in the bio and the caption.
