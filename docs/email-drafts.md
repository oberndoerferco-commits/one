## 2. Welcome series (three letters over ten days)

### 2a. On sign-up

**Subject:** You are on the list
**Preview text:** What that means, and what it does not.

Photograph: the atelier, hands at work.

Thank you for joining the list.

Here is what it means. When a new piece opens on pre-order, you will hear before it is shown
on the site, and you will be able to order first. Once a month you will get one letter from
the atelier: a piece, a commission, a photograph from the bench. That is all. No sales, no
countdowns, nothing sent because it is Tuesday.

If you would like to know how the pieces are made, the next two letters cover it: the
materials and the hands, then the piece that started the house. If you would rather just
wait for the pre-orders, that is fine too.

[Button: See what is made now]

### 2b. Three days later

**Subject:** What it is made of, and who makes it
**Preview text:** Two questions we ask of every piece.

Photograph: a hide and brass fittings, close.

Every piece starts from the same two questions: what is this material, and who is making it
by hand.

The leather is full-grain, from tanneries in Italy, France and Germany: the outer layer of
the hide, left uncorrected, so the grain you see is the grain the animal had. It is the
layer that lasts, and the one that takes on a patina rather than wearing out.

The hardware is solid brass, finished in palladium or gold, chosen because it can be
repaired and re-plated in twenty years rather than replaced.

The making happens in small ateliers around Milan, to order, in small batches, by people
who have done this for most of their lives. There is no factory floor and no stock room.
That is why most pieces are made to order, and why we would rather tell you the truth
about a lead time than keep a warehouse.

The full account, hide by hide, is on the site.

[Button: Materials & Craftsmanship]

### 2c. Ten days after sign-up

**Subject:** The trunk that became a table
**Preview text:** Where the house began, and where the pieces are now.

Photograph: the trunk in the Miramare's rooms.

The first piece we made for a room rather than a person was a trunk, built for Miramare The
Palace in Sanremo, on the Ligurian coast. It was meant to travel. It stayed, and became a
table, and the hotel's public rooms are furnished with what followed.

Our pieces are on show there now, and for sale: a small series made for the hotel is
available there and nowhere else. If you are on the coast, it is the best way to see the
work before deciding on anything.

Everything else is on the site, made to order and sent free with DHL Express wherever you
are. The T-shirts and hoodies are the simplest way in: 240g combed cotton jersey, made in
Italy, on pre-order now.

[Button: Where to Find Us]
