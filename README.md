# one

Working repository for **Oberndörfer Milano** (oberndoerferco.com) — leather
goods: bags in exotic skins, trunks, watch boxes, small leather goods, and
Home & Living. Brand line: *"Handmade in Milan. Made for a lifetime."*

## Supplier sourcing

Research into producers in and around Milan, in answer to four questions asked
in order: find a packaging producer like gboxsrl.it near Milan; find leather
goods producers; expand to 50 producers in and around Milan including Vigevano
and Pavia (clarified as bags, trunks and astucci); then expand the packaging
side in more detail.

### Verification discipline

Every row is labelled. **Verified** means the company's own site or a confirmed
company profile was read, and the description is theirs. **Register lead** means
the firm appears in a chamber-of-commerce or trade directory under that trade at
that address, but the fit is unchecked — some will be repairers or retailers.
A lead is not a recommendation and should not be presented as one.

### Key findings

**Packaging.** G.Box — the reference the founder sent — is in Cornate d'Adda
(MB), founded 1991, ~2,000 m², 6–9 staff, ~€2M revenue; boxes, couvettes and
precious caskets for perfume, cosmetics, fashion and champagne; in a joint
venture with Giovanardi Spa since 2011. Its closest equivalents are Kartolab
(Cusano Milanino), L'Ottagono (Biassono), Montanelli 3 (Burago di Molgora) and
Faenza Couture (Rodano). **Faenza Couture is the only house that publishes a
minimum — 1 to 1,000 pieces at the same quality, same-day prototypes.** That
single fact will probably decide the supplier choice.

**Trunks and watch boxes.** Best match is Aldo Martignoni (Paderno Dugnano),
120 years old, has made for Cartier, Chanel, Bulgari, YSL, Ferrari and L'Oréal.
Then Piatti Milano (in the city since 1953, makes watch winders) and Emilio
Scolari (Brugherio, since 1922).

**Fitted cases.** Confalonieri (Milano, since 1889 — wooden cases for watches,
jewellery and eyewear, leather or fabric covered, satin or velvet interiors)
and Ve.Lia (Rozzano, since 1956, 600+ proprietary moulds, injection moulding,
thermoforming, wood cases, flocked interiors, ISO 9001:2015).

**Exotic skins.** Varese Rettili Pelletteria (Varese, 40 years) is the only
workshop in the region built around reptile skins. The skins themselves come
from Zuma Pelli Pregiate or La Patrie. Ostrich needs no CITES certificate, only
a declaration of origin; alligator and crocodile need papers at every step.

**Geography.** Brianza (Seregno, Giussano, Lissone, Limbiate) holds the
bag-workshop density. Milan holds the trunk and case makers. Vigevano was the
world's shoe capital but its finished-goods makers have thinned — its value now
is ISEM, making luxury rigid boxes for Coty, L'Oréal, Shiseido and LVMH.

**Flagged.** Stilema/Stilux keeps a Milan showroom but manufactures in the Far
East — not a made-in-Italy option.

**Excluded on purpose.** Tuscany and the Marche, which is where the depth of
contract leather work and the low-minimum private-label houses actually are —
but nothing from there supports "Handmade in Milan."

### Open questions

1. **Which craft moves first, and at what quantity per run?** Under ~500 units
   the field collapses to Faenza Couture and Kartolab; above ~5,000 it is
   Pozzoli and ISEM.
2. **Must "Handmade in Milan" stay literally true?** If not, a Tuscan shortlist
   should be added.
3. Offered and not yet taken up: drafting the Italian RFQ emails, and verifying
   a chosen subset of the register leads by phone.
